Privacy Policy — Roll Seven

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Roll Seven ("the App") is a free entertainment app developed by Rahul B. Gaikwad, an individual based in Mumbai, India, doing business as "RCube" ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and what choices you have. It describes the App as it works today.

Information the App collects

  • Anonymous account ID. The App signs you in anonymously using Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier for your install. We do not ask for, or collect, your email address, phone number, or real name.
  • Game progress and settings. Your coin and gem balances, streaks, stage progress, "My Life" purchases, cosmetic choices, sound and vibration settings, and similar game data are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so your progress is saved.
  • Information you choose to show other players. The display name you pick, your avatar, the country flag you choose (if any), your team name, gifts you send, and game statistics such as biggest win, win streak, and stages cleared may be shown to other players in the live feed, leaderboards, teams, and online matches. Please don't use your real name or personal details as your display name.
  • Online matches. When you play online, your anonymous ID, display name, bets and results for that match are processed by our game server (Firebase Cloud Functions) to run the match fairly.
  • Referral codes. If you share or redeem a referral code, the code and which anonymous account redeemed it are stored to award the bonus.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, a push notification token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging is stored so we can send reminders about your own streaks, bonuses and gifts. Reminder notifications scheduled on your device stay on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, Google Firebase automatically processes technical information such as your IP address and device/app details to deliver the service and keep it secure, as described in Firebase's privacy information.

How we use it

Only to run the game: saving your progress, showing leaderboards and the live feed, running online matches, delivering gifts and referral bonuses, and sending the notifications you opted into. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the App contains no third-party advertising and no in-app purchases. Coins and gems are earned only by playing. If ads or optional purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, naming the services used and what they collect.

Virtual currency

Coins and gems have no cash value. They cannot be bought with, redeemed for, or exchanged into real money, goods, or prizes.

Sharing

Your information is processed by Google (Firebase) as our service provider, and the items you choose to display are visible to other players as described above. We do not share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data is kept for as long as you play. Because accounts are anonymous, uninstalling the App ends access to that account. To have your data deleted from our servers, email us at rg4640020@gmail.com with your in-game display name, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in Google Firebase, which restricts each player to reading and writing only their own private data.

Age requirement

Roll Seven includes simulated gambling and is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
