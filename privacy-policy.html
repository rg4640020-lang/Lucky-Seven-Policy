Privacy Policy — Lucky Seven

Last updated: July 11,2026

Lucky Seven ("the App") is a free entertainment app developed by Rahul B. Gaikwad. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it's used.

What we collect

The App uses Firebase (Google) for anonymous sign-in and to store your in-game progress (coin balance, gems, streak, team membership). No name, email, phone number, or personal identity is collected — each install is assigned an anonymous device identifier only.

Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded video ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and general usage data to serve and measure ads, per Google's advertising policy. You are never required to watch an ad to play the game.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties beyond Google/Firebase/AdMob's standard service operation.
  • We don't collect real names, emails, or payment information — the App has no in-app purchases.
  • In-game coins and gems have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money, goods, or prizes.

Children

Lucky Seven is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program. It includes simulated gambling mechanics and is intended for users 18 and older.

Data deletion

Since accounts are anonymous, uninstalling the App removes your local session. To request deletion of any associated data, contact us at rg4640020@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
