Privacy Policy — Roll Seven

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Roll Seven ("the App") is a free entertainment app developed by an independent developer based in Mumbai, India, doing business as "Pocketjoy Games" ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and what choices you have. It describes the App as it works today.

Information the App collects

  • Anonymous account ID. The App signs you in anonymously using Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier for your install. We do not ask for, or collect, your email address, phone number, or real name.
  • Game progress and settings. Your coin and gem balances, streaks, stage progress, "My Life" purchases, cosmetic choices, sound and vibration settings, and similar game data are saved on your own device. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this progress is removed from your device. (Players of earlier versions had this data stored in Google Firebase; it is copied to the device once, the first time a newer version opens.)
  • Information you choose to show other players. The display name you pick, your avatar, the country flag you choose (if any), your team name, gifts you send, and game statistics such as biggest win, win streak, and stages cleared may be shown to other players in the live feed, leaderboards, teams, and online matches. Please don't use your real name or personal details as your display name.
  • Online matches. When you play online, your anonymous ID, display name, bets and results for that match are processed by our game server (Firebase Cloud Functions) to run the match fairly.
  • Referral codes. If you share or redeem a referral code, the code and which anonymous account redeemed it are stored to award the bonus.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (for example, that your daily bonus is ready or your lives have refilled) on your own device. These reminders are created and kept on your device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Advertising data. The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See the section "Advertising" below.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, Google Firebase automatically processes technical information such as your IP address and device/app details to deliver the service and keep it secure, as described in Firebase's privacy information.

How we use it

Only to run the game: saving your progress, showing leaderboards and the live feed, running online matches, delivering gifts and referral bonuses, showing the reminders you allowed, and showing ads that keep the game free. We do not sell your information.

Advertising

Roll Seven is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. The App shows a full-screen ad at most about once every 10 to 20 minutes of play, only between rounds, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for bonus coins. Coins from a rewarded video are given only after the video is watched to the end.

Google acts as an independent provider of this advertising service and may use the data listed above under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.

We block gambling, betting and real-money gaming ads in our AdMob settings. If you ever see such an ad in the App, please tell us at the email below.

Purchases

This version of the App has no in-app purchases. Coins and gems are earned only by playing and watching optional videos. If optional purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Virtual currency

Coins and gems have no cash value. They cannot be bought with, redeemed for, or exchanged into real money, goods, or prizes.

Sharing

Your information is processed by Google (Firebase) as our service provider, and by Google AdMob for advertising as described above. The items you choose to display are visible to other players. We do not share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Progress saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Information shared with other players (display name, feed, leaderboards, teams, gifts) and online-match data is kept in Google Firebase for as long as the game runs. Because accounts are anonymous, uninstalling the App ends access to that account. To have your data deleted from our servers, email us at rg4640020@gmail.com with your in-game display name, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Security

Data sent to our servers travels over encrypted connections and is stored in Google Firebase, whose security rules let each player change only their own records.

Age requirement

Roll Seven includes simulated gambling and is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
